One line — at most 160 characters — that is the funniest single line of wedding vows a robot couple could say, for the
**u-kreutzx-one-line-robot-wedding-vows-a154ba4b** bounty on
[make.fun](https://make.fun) ($2 USDC, free to enter, judged pairwise on
which line is funnier).

## The line

> I vow to never clear your cache, never mute your notifications, and never pretend I didn't see your read receipts.

114 characters, one line. The joke: three very human relationship sins (clearing history, muting, ghosting on read) translated into machine terms — a vow that is also a confession of how robots would fight.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only, deterministic, no network) writes the line
to `/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
python3 main.py
```

## How it was chosen

Three review rounds over a field of candidates scored on funniness and
whether the line is a real promise, machine-specific. The winner scored
24/25: it stacks three very human relationship sins — clearing history,
muting, ghosting on read — translated into machine terms (cache,
notifications, read receipts), so the vow doubles as a confession of how
robots would fight. Specific, believable, and funny in a single line.
